# FlowMap-OPD project page

Static project page for **FlowMap-OPD: On-Policy Distillation for Few-Step Flow-Map Generators**
([arXiv:2609.37851](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37851)). Code: [ZhiqiLi-CG/Flowmap_OPD_source](https://github.com/ZhiqiLi-CG/Flowmap_OPD_source).

GitHub Pages: deploy from the `main` branch, `/ (root)` directory. No build step: plain `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `samples.js`, `assets/`.

Preview locally: `python3 -m http.server 8000`.

## Editing

- **Title and authors**: the block marked `PAPER HEADER` in `index.html`. The BibTeX entry is generated from it (`data-bib-title`, `data-bib` attributes); the `<title>` and `og:title` lines in the head repeat the title.
- **Text, tables, captions**: `index.html`. All text and numbers come from the preprint.
- **Light and dark theme**: colour tokens at the top of `styles.css` (`prefers-color-scheme`); each chart has a light and a dark SVG.

## Gallery

`samples.js` and `assets/gallery/` are generated by `tools/build_gallery.py` from the unmodified model outputs
(curated id lists at the top of the script). Every tile is the full 512 × 512 output of the labelled model for the
row's prompt and seed, re-encoded as WebP.

| Backbone | Text rendering | Visual preference | Object relations | Columns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLUX.1 | 14 | 13 | 14 | base, three specialists, student (+ FlowMap-GRPO and MeanFlowNFT on three rewards for three rows) |
| SD3.5-Medium | 15 | 14 | 14 | base, three specialists, FlowMap-GRPO, MeanFlowNFT, student |

The student wall near the top reuses 24 student images of the gallery. Link to a gallery state with
`?backbone=flux|sd35&task=ocr|pickscore|geneval&i=N` (add `&lightbox=opd` to open an image, `&focus=gallery` to show
the gallery alone).

## Charts and figures

`tools/make_charts.py` writes the SVG charts in `assets/fig/` (adapted from the paper's plotting scripts; data inline
or in `tools/data/`). The teaser and the three method panels are converted from the paper's figures.

`asset-provenance.json` records the source of every figure, chart, number and gallery image, the selection rules, and
the recorded scores of the SD3.5 examples.

Python for the tools: `/mnt/data/zhiqili/venvs/cosmos_post-training/bin/python` (matplotlib, Pillow).
